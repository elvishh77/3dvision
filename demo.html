Does it follow the hands?

Held-out clips the model never trained on. Every panel gets the same first frame and prompt. The base model and the trained model generate the rest of the video, and the trained model also receives the hand trajectory.

From human hands to robot hands

HRDexDB (xArm + Inspire hand, and human hands) and SharpaDex (bimanual Sharpa Wave hands): the robot's own joint trajectory (forward kinematics) is drawn into the same hand-conditioning volume as a human hand

Same interface, new embodiment

The robot hand's FK fills the same 21 keypoints and 778 surface points a MANO hand would, with contact = surface within 10 mm of the object. The encoder is unchanged.

What each model saw

1.3B models (mix 15k, camera + LoRA): HOT3D + HRDexDB human clips, no robot data. Wan2.2 A14B adapter (v4, 5k steps, high-noise expert only, no camera): HOT3D + HRDexDB human + SharpaDex robot episodes (episodes 390-409 held out). No model ever saw the Inspire hand, and 12 HRDexDB objects are held out entirely.
