Do the hands go where we asked?

How a generated video is scored: detect the hands in every frame, compare them with the trajectory the model was given, and ask a vision-language model whether the prompted outcome happened.

The pipeline

Run per checkpoint by eval/run_eval.py on one GPU
1

Inputs

A held-out clip's first frame, outcome prompt and real MANO hand trajectory with contact.
2

Generate

The model makes a 49-frame video from those inputs (about 67 s).
3

Detect hands

WiLoR finds hands, HaMeR gives 21 keypoints per hand and HACO gives the parts in contact, in every frame.
4

Ask the judge

Qwen2.5-VL-7B answers yes/no questions built from the prompt, at 8 keyframes.
5

Score

Detected vs conditioned hands, per hand and per clip, are averaged over the set. Overlay videos are written for checking by eye.

The judge's checklist (base model's video)

Questions about other objects in the scene are controls: the right answer is "no". On a single clip the judge often says yes to those too, so task accuracy is only reported pooled over all 32 clips, never per clip.

Results so far

Held-out check during training (no video generated)

Every 500 steps the training job measures how much worse the model's prediction gets on 8 held-out clips when the hand conditioning is changed. Positive = the model relies on that part of the conditioning.

In progress: the one-hand test

Approved Nov 13; critic-evaluator is building and validating it

Question

If only one hand is told to act, does only that hand act? The current eval never gives the model a trajectory different from the real one.

Setup

18 eval clips with both hands active. Each clip is generated three times: both hands, right hand only and left hand only. In the one-hand runs the other hand is held still at its first-frame pose with no contact.

Score

For the active hand: trajectory error and contact. For the idle hand: does it stay still and empty? Headline: contact rate of the commanded hand minus that of the idle hand.
Why it matters: the held-out check shows the model barely reacts when the left and right hands are swapped (+0.002). It may follow where the hands are drawn rather than which hand is which.
