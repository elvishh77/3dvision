SharpaDex: held-out robot episodes

A bimanual robot (two Sharpa Wave hands) assembles gears. Episodes 390-409 were held out of training: 130 clips of 49 frames. Every model gets the real first frame and prompt; the hand adapter also gets the robot's hand trajectory (forward kinematics drawn into the same volume as a human hand).
