Model architecture

Inputs on the left, output on the right. Dashed = frozen, blue = trained (432M parameters on top of the frozen 1.56B DiT).

Why it starts as the base model

All projections between the adapter and the base are zero-initialised. At step 0 the adapter adds exactly nothing, so training only has to learn what the hands add.

What is trained

The patch-embed, the 9 zero-init projections and 8 copied transformer blocks (432M parameters). The VAE, CLIP, T5 and the 30-block DiT (1.56B) stay frozen.

Training cost

Encoders run once offline (21 GB latent cache), so a step only touches the DiT and the adapter. That's about 6 s per 2 clips on an RTX 6000 Ada, or 1.2 s per 8 clips on 8× H100.
